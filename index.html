About Maruti

Material Truth. Engineered Form.

We are a trusted manufacturer of high-quality metal hardware components including hinges, knobs, handles, and fittings. Our focus is precision engineering, durability, and modern design.

Every component that leaves our facility has been machined to tolerances measured in microns, tested under extreme conditions, and finished to reveal the inherent beauty of raw metal.

MISSION

Deliver high-quality hardware solutions with precision manufacturing and reliable performance. Every piece machined to exacting standards.

VISION

To become a global leader in innovative hardware manufacturing, setting the standard for quality, durability, and material excellence.
